Our Story

TN Real Estate is a new property and accommodation service that connects people with homes, rooms, offices and event spaces to rent or buy. We started with a simple idea: finding a place to live, stay or celebrate should not mean searching five different websites.

Mission & Vision

To make it simple to find and enquire about homes, rooms, hotels and event venues, and to give owners an easy way to list their properties.

To become South Africa's most trusted place to find a property, a room, a stay or a venue, known for honest listings and friendly, reliable service.
